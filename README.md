# Kinetix
Kinetix is a program that helps you use your PC when your hands are dirty.
It controls your mouse wia simple hand gestures, for instance, if you want to left click, you just pinch your middle finger and thumb.

---

## For now we have working:
- Mouse movement *(index and thumb pinch)*
- Left and right mouse clicks *(middle/ring and thumb)*
- Scroll *(index and middle together)*
- Play/pause for media *(fist)*
