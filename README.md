# Kinetix
Kinetix is a program that helps you use your PC when your hands dirty.
It controls your mouse wia simple hand gestures, for example when you want to left click you just pinch your middle finger and thumb.

---

For now we have working:
- Mouse movement (index and thumb pinch)
- Left and right mouse clicks (middle/ring and thumb)
- Scroll (index and middle together)
- Play/pause for media (fist)
